## How to organise Research notes

- **How the app shows this note:** the page's **Details** card shows type, course, date and status, and **Relates to** lists the notes in `related` plus the notes that link back to it. Link the Items notes this one draws on through `related`; Zotero items also show their collections.
- One note per question, filed in the top-level `Research` folder and named `{Course} - Research - {Topic}`.
- It is a working brief, not a submission: never copy its sentences into assessed work. Cite the original sources.
- Every claim has a link. If it cannot be verified, it goes under Gaps, not Findings.
- `summary` is a one-line statement of the answer, so the folder's table is scannable.
