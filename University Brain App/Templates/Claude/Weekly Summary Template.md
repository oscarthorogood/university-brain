## How to organise Weekly Summary notes

- One note per teaching week, written on the Friday or Sunday, covering Monday to Sunday.
- Title pattern (proposed): `Weekly Summary - Year {N} S{N} Week {NN}`, e.g. `Weekly Summary - Year 3 S1 Week 03`. Zero-pad the week number and put the date in `date` rather than the title.
- `date` is the Monday the week starts; `Week No.` is the Edinburgh teaching week, so summaries sort in order. Use `related` for the Lecture, Tutorial, Reading, Revision and Internship notes the week covers.
- There is no `course` field: a week spans several courses, so courses are linked inside **Courses this week** instead.
- Keep `summary` to one line naming the week's main themes.

**Pulling detail from the vault**
- Each per-item section (Lectures, Tutorials, Readings, Essays & projects, Revision) gets one block per note; copy the block as many times as needed and delete any section with no items that week.
- Build **Courses this week** from the notes whose `date` or `due` falls in the week — Lectures, Tutorials, Essays, Projects and Revision — and the TaskNotes tasks dated that week.
- Copy **Deadlines** from the course notes' Key dates and Calendar Sync; cite the source note. Leave a line blank rather than guess.
