## How to organise Lecture notes

- **How the app shows links:** the note page's **Related** card lists every Revision, Research, Zotero, Resources or OneDrive item this note links to (through `resources`, `onedrive`, or any `[[link]]` in the text) or that links back to it. **Links to** lists the other Items notes it links to (through `related`, `readings` or any `[[link]]`). Properties (course, date, status, tags) are edited on the page. Link things once, with a wikilink; don't repeat them by hand.
- One note per lecture, filed in the top-level `Lectures` folder — not nested under the course note.
- Always link `course:: [[Course Name]]` so the lecture is grouped correctly in course-grouped Bases views and appears under the course's Linked mentions.
- Fill in `Lecture No.` so lectures sort in delivery order within a course, even if notes are created out of order. `date` is the delivery date; the time and room go in **Session details**.
- Link any slides, recordings, or handouts via `resources`, and any assigned readings via `readings`, rather than pasting content inline.
- Use `related` for links to other lectures the content builds on or connects to (e.g. a follow-up or prerequisite lecture).
- Keep `summary` to a one-line topic description so a course's lecture list is scannable at a glance.

**Notes section format**
- The 📝 Notes section follows Oscar's own note style inside a `> [!note]+ Lecture notes` callout (like every other section): plain-text headings, plain `* ` bullets, dense one-fact-per-bullet detail with (Author, Year) citations, `Note:` bullets for distinctions, indented numbered sub-lists. Cover the whole lecture, section by section.

**Pulling detail from attachments**
- Read every linked file in full before writing: slides give the structure, objectives, definitions, frameworks and examples; handouts and transcripts give detail the slides skip.
- The slide deck's own title slide beats the filename for the lecture topic — use it to rename a placeholder title like `Lecture 3`.
- Cite a location for every extracted point (`slide 12`, `p. 4`, `12:30`). If something isn't in the attachments, leave the field blank rather than guessing.
