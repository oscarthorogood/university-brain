## How to organise MCQ notes

- **How the app shows this note:** the page's **Details** card shows type, course, date and status, and **Relates to** lists the notes in `related` plus the notes that link back to it. Link the Items notes this one draws on through `related`.
- Name it `{Course Name} - MCQ - {Topic}`. Link `Course:: [[Course Name]]` (capital C on this type) and set `date` to when it was made or is due.
- `type` is one of: `MCQ Test`, `Quiz`.
- One note per question set, filed in `Apps/MCQ`.
- Questions follow the format above exactly; the app turns them into a clickable quiz. Keep the explanation to one line and cite the slide or note.
- Set `type` to `MCQ Test` (a full set) or `Quiz` (a short one).
- Keep `status` current (`Not started` → `In Progress` → `Done`).
